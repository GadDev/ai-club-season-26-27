# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want the AI Club project to be a welcoming, useful, and technically serious space for people with different experience levels, backgrounds, and perspectives.

Everyone participating in this repository is expected to help create an environment that is respectful, constructive, and safe.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a healthy community include:

- giving and receiving technical feedback with respect;
- disagreeing with ideas without attacking people;
- explaining context rather than assuming prior knowledge;
- welcoming beginners while keeping advanced discussion rigorous;
- crediting other people's work and sources;
- respecting privacy, confidentiality, and intellectual-property boundaries;
- accepting reasonable moderation decisions and correcting mistakes in good faith.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language;
- personal insults, sustained hostility, or deliberate humiliation;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- knowingly submitting plagiarized, confidential, malicious, or unlawfully obtained material;
- repeatedly disrupting discussions or reviews after being asked to stop;
- retaliation against somebody who reports a conduct concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project-related events, and other spaces where somebody is representing this project.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the repository maintainer through a private channel where possible. If no private channel is available, open a minimal issue asking for a private conduct contact **without including sensitive details**.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues, or other contributions that violate this policy. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Reports will be handled as discreetly as reasonably possible. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, repeated behaviour, and the safety of participants.

## Good-faith technical disagreement

Strong technical disagreement is welcome. Criticism of architecture, implementation, sources, or product decisions is not a conduct violation when it remains focused on the work and is expressed professionally.

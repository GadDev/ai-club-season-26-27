## Editorial rationale

- Every month offers at least one entry point in each track. Foundations introduces mental models and tangible interfaces; Engineering develops implementations and evaluation; Deep Dive examines architecture and operation.
- The eight placements from the first board remain in their original months and tracks: P01, P10, P02, P14, P15, P19, P20, and P05.
- P25 is framed as a controlled **follow-up lab** to the existing agent-security material. P07 focuses on runtime design and mission boundaries, avoiding another generic introduction to agents.
- October, December, and March each contain four pairs (eight proposed sessions). The remaining months contain three pairs (six sessions). This is a capacity question for organizers, not a fixed cadence.

# Constitution

1. **The programme is the product.** The tool supports good sessions and cannot consume the energy needed to make them.
2. **Show the whole season honestly.** Publish proposals early, but never portray an unconfirmed date or speaker as booked.
3. **Keep the tracks parallel.** Foundations, Engineering, and Deep Dive are concurrent entry points, not grades or sequential stages.
4. **Separate dimensions.** Track is audience; domain is subject; tags add detail; format describes teaching; date is schedule; status conveys certainty and lifecycle.
5. **Be rigorous at every level.** Beginner assumes no prior knowledge of this subject. Advanced depth must serve a meaningful problem.
6. **Prefer practice and critical thinking.** Use examples, demonstrations, exercises, failure analysis, and trade-offs; avoid hype.
7. **Structure before automation.** Curate real sessions before building dashboards or workflows. A programme gap is a signal, not an invitation to invent an event.
8. **Keep content portable and accountable.** Use standard files, stable links, source and presenter credit, and reviewable corrections.
9. **Build accessibility into V1.** Keyboard access, readable text, sufficient contrast, and a list equivalent to the visual board are required. Never rely on colour alone.
10. **Respect people.** Avoid attendance surveillance, streaks, leaderboards, and personal scores. Recognize presenters professionally.
11. **Protect editorial independence.** Future Institute links should be contextual and clearly labelled, not advertisements disguised as sessions.
12. **Let learning paths emerge.** Add prerequisites, assessments, or credentials only when actual content and participant needs justify them.
13. **Prefer the smallest maintainable system.** A static site and Git-managed content suffice for V1 unless evidence changes the decision.

**Review question:** Does a proposed feature make the programme easier to plan, understand, discover, or learn from? Record the reason for any exception to these principles.
